============================================================================
RELATIONSHIP LEGEND
============================================================================

    ─── 1       One
    ─── M       Many
    ─── FK      Foreign Key
    PK          Primary Key

============================================================================
RELATIONSHIP LIST
============================================================================

    1.  departments(1) ────M employees           (department has many employees)
    2.  employees(1) ──────M visits               (employee hosts many visits)
    3.  visitors(1) ───────M visits               (visitor makes many visits)
    4.  employees(1) ──────M employee_schedules   (employee has many schedule entries)
    5.  visitors(1) ───────M appointments         (visitor makes many appointments)
    6.  employees(1) ──────M appointments         (employee receives many appointments)
    7.  appointments(1) ───M notifications        (appointment triggers notifications)
    8.  employees(1) ──────M notifications        (employee receives notifications)
    9.  appointments(1) ───M email_queue          (appointment triggers emails)
    10. appointments(1) ───M sms_queue            (appointment triggers SMS)
    11. users(1) ──────────M audit_logs           (user creates audit entries)
