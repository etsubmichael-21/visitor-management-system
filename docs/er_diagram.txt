================================================================================
KEY RELATIONSHIP RULES
================================================================================

  - RESTRICT: visitors / employees / departments cannot be deleted while they
    have visits, appointments, or audit records referencing them.
  - CASCADE: deleting an appointment removes its properties, comments,
    attachments and reschedule requests; deleting a user removes their
    refresh_tokens; deleting an employee removes schedules, unavailability and
    notifications.
  - SET NULL: deleting an appointment clears appointment_id on visits,
    notifications and visitor_notifications; deleting a property clears
    checkout_items.appointment_property_id; deleting a user clears
    reschedule_requests.responded_by_user_id.
  - email_queue / sms_queue / appointments.assigned_by carry no FK — they are
    application-managed references to keep queue jobs independent of cleanup.
